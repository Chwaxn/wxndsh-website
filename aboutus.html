ABOUT US

WXNDSH was founded with one clear purpose: to make discovering and creating events easier, faster and more meaningful for everyone. The founder of the website WXNDSH is Mr. Che Wan Muhammad Danish Bin Che Wan Mohd Azlizan. He is currently a student in Digital Media Communication Technology at Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM), Alor Gajah Campus, Melaka. Originally from the state of Terengganu, he now resides in the Kemaman district.
